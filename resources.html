Browsing Safely

About the Quick Exit button

Every page on this site has a Quick Exit button in the top corner. One click instantly leaves this site and replaces it in your browser history, so it won't show up if you hit "back." You can also press Esc three times quickly to trigger it from anywhere.

If you're worried someone may check your device, consider browsing from a public or borrowed computer, and clear your browser history afterward.

Applying for support

To ask about an Ascendra Rise Grant or the Rise & Rebuild Program, reach out through our Contact page. A member of our team will follow up to talk through eligibility and next steps.

You can also ask a case worker, advocate, or partner organization to refer you directly.
