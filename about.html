About Ascendra

Our Mission & Vision

Everything we build — grants, coaching, community — starts from the same premise: independence is possible when survivors have real resources behind them.

Mission

Ascendra Foundation empowers survivors of domestic violence to rebuild safe and independent lives through financial education, career development, entrepreneurship support, and economic empowerment programs.

Vision

A future where survivors of domestic violence have the financial resources, career opportunities, and community support needed to build independent and thriving lives.

Our Approach

Independence, built in stages

We start from the belief that every survivor deserves the resources and support to build a life of safety and independence — not just crisis intervention, but a real path forward.

That's why Ascendra pairs immediate financial relief with longer-term coaching and career development: short-term grants move survivors past urgent barriers, while structured mentorship and entrepreneurship support carry that momentum into lasting stability.
